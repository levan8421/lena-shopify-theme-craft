🏗 Architecture Notes — Developer Handoff

This prototype represents the recommended website architecture for Lena Handicrafts, based on competitive analysis of 10+ artisan e-commerce sites and the existing business model. Below are implementation notes for Claude Code or a Shopify developer.

Shopify Theme

  • Recommended base: Craft (free Shopify theme) — includes Lookbook, Story, and Press sections natively
  • Alternative: Studio (free) for gallery-style grid, or Highlight ($300) for asymmetric layouts
  • Custom CSS variables above can be ported directly into Shopify theme base.css

Navigation Structure

  • 5 top-level items max: This Week's Drop | The Gallery | Our Story | Find Us | Cart
  • The Gallery is a dropdown: Crochet Figures / Compact Mirrors / Hats & Headbands / Purses / Sale
  • Remove all "#MK" suffixes and "Online Collections" vs "Market Collections" distinction

Collections (Shopify)

  • This Week's Drop — Automated: tag = drop-current OR created within last 7 days + available for sale
  • Available Now — Automated: inventory > 0
  • Crochet Figures — Tag-based: crochet-figure
  • Compact Mirrors — Tag-based: compact-mirror
  • Hats & Headbands — Tag-based: hat OR headband
  • Signature Purses — Tag-based: purse
  • Sale — Manual: clearance items

Product Card Requirements

  • Always show 1 of 1 badge (top-left)
  • New badge for items created within 7 days
  • Sold-out items stay visible with overlay: "This piece found its home"
  • Sold-out items show "Notify me of similar drops" button (Notify Me app or Klaviyo Back-in-Stock)
  • Card info: category eyebrow → product name → price → scarcity line

Product Page Template

  • Gallery: main photo + 2 detail shots (embroidery close-up, scale/in-hand)
  • Above title: 1 of 1 badge + category eyebrow
  • Below price: artisan attribution line (italic)
  • Scarcity messaging: green dot + "Only piece in existence"
  • Conflict disclaimer below Add to Cart
  • Accordion: Care Instructions, Shipping & Returns
  • Cross-sell: "Other pieces in this week's drop"

Key Apps Needed

  • Shopify Email (free) or Klaviyo (free tier) — for weekly drop emails
  • Notify Me or Back in Stock (free tier) — email capture on sold-out items
  • Optional: Pasilobus Social Proof (free) for "X people viewing" during drops

Email Flow (Klaviyo/Shopify Email)

  • Signup confirmation: "Welcome to the drop list"
  • 24h before drop: "Tomorrow's drop preview — here's what's coming"
  • Drop moment: "It's live — shop this week's drop now"
  • Weekly: "What sold out this week" (FOMO builder)
Key difference from Ganapati Crafts: Ganapati sells repeatable, multi-unit products. Lena sells true one-of-one pieces. This prototype is designed for the drop/gallery model, not the catalog/restock model. Every product card gets a "1 of 1" badge. Sold-out items remain visible as portfolio proof. The newsletter signup promises "first access" not "discounts."

Naming Conventions (for Shopify product titles)

  • Crochet Figures: descriptive name → Pink Bunny with Flower Crown
  • Mirrors/Hats/Headbands: [Base Color] - [Main Design] → Ivory - Rose Garden
  • Photo sells the product; name is a label, not a description

SEO Title Format

  • [Product Type] — [Design Name] | Handmade by Lena Handicrafts
  • Example: Compact Mirror — Rose Garden on Ivory | Handmade by Lena Handicrafts

Responsive Breakpoints

  • Desktop: 4-column product grid, 2-column story/PDP layout
  • Tablet (≤900px): 2-column product grid, single-column story
  • Mobile (≤640px): 2-column product grid (compact), hamburger nav, stacked newsletter form
  • Mobile product card: image + title + "1 of 1" + price only. No hover effects.

Performance Notes

  • Lazy-load all product images below the fold
  • Hero image: serve WebP at 1200px max width
  • Product photos: 800×1000px max, WebP, compressed to <100KB each
  • Target: Lighthouse Performance score ≥ 90
