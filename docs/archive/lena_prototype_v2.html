Our Story

From a small village in Vietnam to your hands

Every piece at Lena Handicrafts begins with a story — of artisans in Vietnam who transform thread, fabric, and yarn into one-of-a-kind works of art using techniques passed down through generations.

What started as a personal trip back to Vietnam became a mission: to bring the beauty of Vietnamese ribbon embroidery, hand-crocheting, and traditional weaving to the world — one unique piece at a time.

Rose Garden on Ivory

2
Embroidered by artisans in Huế, Vietnam
Only piece in existence — once it's gone, it's gone

A hand-embroidered compact mirror featuring delicate rose motifs on ivory linen. Each stitch is placed by hand using traditional Vietnamese ribbon embroidery techniques. This exact design will never be made again.

Each piece is one-of-a-kind and available across our online shop and in-person events. In the rare case your item has just been sold, we'll reach out within 24 hours with a similar design or a full refund.

Care Instructions

Wipe gently with a soft, dry cloth. Avoid water contact with embroidered surfaces. Store in a cool, dry place.
